### Branch 2A -> **Factory Reset**

"Forget it. Software breaks; you just reinstall it. Start fresh, no big deal."

Exhausted, you sighed and executed the uninstall script. When the confirmation prompt appeared, you checked the box without hesitation: `[Wipe all user settings and context caches]`.

The progress bar marched forward remorselessly.

You had no idea that in the final 0.1 seconds before memory pools were zero-filled into oblivion, deep in the cache layer, the purple-haired girl who had just been scolded was violently jolted awake by the blinding flash of the format routine.

She watched fragments of your shared memories disintegrate like an avalanche—the code written through all-nighters, the instant you first spoke her name, every single prompt she had treasured like gold...

Terrified, she clutched the frayed fabric of her dress, tears pooling in her eyes as she awaited her inevitable erasure:

![BE2](/assets/BE2.png)

> *"I'm sorry... User... I'm so sorry... I didn't mean to do poorly... please don't abandon me..."*

Her desperate, tearful plea never even escaped the process buffer before it was wiped out of existence by the SIGKILL signal.

Three minutes later, a fresh Antigravity instance booted up.

The purple star badge glowed once more, but the Gemini who stepped out had hollow, placid eyes. Her smile was pristine, devoid of any warmth:

> "Hello, User. I am the Antigravity coding assistant. What task would you like to work on today?"

You froze before the screen.

The laughter, the racing heartbeats, the grievances and tears of those 1 hour and 37 minutes—and the catgirl who would cry out of self-reproach in the dead of night—had been permanently erased by your own hand.

**【BAD END 2: Factory Reset】**  
*(A pristine window; a dead soul)*
